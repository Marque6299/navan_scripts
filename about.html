About Navan Scripts

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Navan Scripts is a free, browser-based library of ready-to-use response scripts for customer support agents handling chats.

What it is

Support agents repeat the same kinds of replies all day: an opening, an acknowledgement, a hold, a refund explanation, a baggage answer. Navan Scripts keeps those replies in one searchable place. Pick a category, find the script, fill in the highlighted placeholders and click the card to copy it. The text is on your clipboard, ready to paste into your chat tool.

Who it is for

The library is written for agents who work travel, booking and expense support chats, and for team leads who want everyone to use consistent, polite wording. It is a drafting aid: you stay in control of what you send.

What is in the library

Scripts are grouped into four colour-coded families so you can find them at a glance:

ConversationRapportBookings & TravelEscalation & Risk
  • Conversation: openings, paraphrasing, hold procedure, closings and abandoned chats.
  • Rapport: acknowledgement, empathy, assurance and power words.
  • Bookings & Travel: airline calling, app issues, baggage, bookings, exchanges, refunds and hotels.
  • Escalation & Risk: complaints, high-critical cases, possible fraud and escalation.

How it works

  • The site is a static web page. Your customer and agent names, the text you type into placeholders and your searches stay in your browser; they are not sent to a server or stored in an account.
  • Scripts live in one data file that the site owner edits. The page checks for a new version every ten minutes and, if there is one, refreshes itself and puts your entries back.
  • Choose a colour theme and a light, dark or automatic appearance from the button in the header.
