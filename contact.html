What to write to us about

  • A script correction. Include the category, the script title and what should change.
  • A suggestion. A missing situation, a clearer wording, a new category.
  • A technical problem. Tell us your browser and device, what you clicked and what you expected. Please do not send customer information.
  • A privacy request. Questions about the Privacy Policy, or a request to access or delete data. The site does not keep accounts, so most data lives only in your browser, but we will help where we can.
  • An advertising concern. If an ad looks inappropriate, tell us the advertiser or what the ad showed so we can block it in our ad settings.
  • Content or trademark concerns. If you own material that appears on the site and want it reviewed or removed, tell us what and where.

Before you write

The Help page answers most usage questions. The About page explains what the site is and how it is run.
