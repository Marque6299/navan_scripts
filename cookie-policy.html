Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This page lists the cookies and similar browser storage the site and its partners use, and how to control them.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as local storage and session storage, work the same way. Some are essential to how a page behaves, others measure usage or support advertising.

What the site uses

NameSet byPurposeType and duration
navan.theme, navan.modeThis siteRemember your colour theme and light / dark / auto choiceLocal storage, until cleared. Functional
navan.session.snapshot, navan.session.lastReloadThis siteKeep your entries safe while the page refreshes itself for an updateSession storage, seconds to the end of the tab. Functional

Ad cookies such as __gads, __gpi and othersGoogle AdSense and its partnersDeliver and measure ads, limit repeats, personalise where allowed, prevent fraudThird-party cookies, duration set by Google. Advertising

The names and lifetimes of Google cookies are controlled by Google and can change; see Google’s cookie information.

Your choices

  • Consent message. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before analytics and advertising cookies are used. To change your decision, use the Privacy choices link in the footer of the main page. If no message appears there, your region does not require one.
  • Ad personalisation. Turn it off in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of interest-based ads at aboutads.info.
  • Analytics. Install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on, or enable “Do Not Track” in your browser.
  • Browser settings. Every major browser lets you block or delete cookies and site data (see your browser’s Help). Blocking functional storage only means the theme is not remembered and entries may not survive an automatic refresh.
