Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This policy explains what information Navan Scripts (“we”, “the site”) handles when you use it, and the choices you have.

1. The short version

  • The site has no accounts and no database of users. The names and text you type into the customer, agent, placeholder and search fields stay in your browser.
  • We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used, and Google AdSense to show advertising that pays for it. Those Google services use cookies and similar technologies.
  • Please do not type confidential customer details (card numbers, passwords and similar) into the site.

This information is never sent to us or to any third party by the site’s own code.

3. Google Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-EEHXSE9PG0), a service of Google LLC. It records page views and a few interactions: which category you open, which script title you copy, how many results a search returned, and theme changes. It also receives standard technical data such as approximate location derived from your IP address, browser, device type and referring page. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store full IP addresses.

We do not send the customer name, the agent name, the text of your searches or the text of scripts to Google Analytics. The site honours the browser “Do Not Track” setting for its own interaction events. You can also install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. How Google uses data from sites that use its services is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. Advertising (Google AdSense)

The site shows ads from Google AdSense (publisher ID ca-pub-6978764838614552). Google and its advertising partners use cookies, mobile advertising IDs and similar technologies to serve and measure ads, limit how often you see an ad, and, where permitted, personalise ads based on your interests. Ads are labelled “Advertisement” and appear between script cards and at the end of the page.

  • Manage ad personalisation in Google Ads Settings.
  • Learn about interest-based advertising and opt out through aboutads.info (US) or youronlinechoices.com (Europe).
  • Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent message from Google before ad cookies are used. You can reopen it at any time with the Privacy choices link in the page footer (see also the Cookie Policy).

5. Hosting and server logs

The site is delivered by a web hosting provider. Like any web server, it may keep technical logs (IP address, requested page, time, browser) for security and reliability. We do not use these logs to identify individual visitors.

6. Legal bases (EEA, UK and similar laws)

Where those laws apply, we rely on consent for advertising and analytics cookies, and on our legitimate interest in running a secure, working site for the storage described in section 2. You can withdraw consent at any time through the Privacy choices link or your browser settings.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of personal data, to object to its use, to data portability, and to complain to your data protection authority. California residents may also ask what is collected and opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for advertising. We do not sell personal information. To make a request, see the Contact page.

8. Children

The site is intended for working adults and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Third-party links and international transfers

The site may link to other websites; their privacy practices are their own. Google may process data in countries other than your own, under the safeguards described in Google’s own policies.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how the site handles information, we will update this page and its “Last updated” date.
