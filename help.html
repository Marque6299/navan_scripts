Help and user guide

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Everything you need to find a script, personalise it and copy it into your chat in a few seconds.

1. Find a script

  • Browse by category. The coloured buttons under the header are the categories. Click one to show its scripts. The current category has a white ring around it.
  • See every category. The rail shows two rows. If there are more categories, a +N button appears at the right end. Hover over the rail (mouse) or press the button (touch or keyboard) to open the rest; it folds back when you pick one.
  • Search. Type in the search box (press / to jump to it). Search looks at script titles first, then at the script text. Every word you type has to match. Press Esc or the × button to clear it.

Colours are a visual aid only: blue is conversation flow, green is rapport, amber is bookings and travel, red is escalation and risk, purple is anything new that has not been grouped yet. Hover over a button to see its family.

2. Personalise

  • Type the customer’s name and your own name in the header boxes. Every [Customer Name] and [Agent Name] in the library fills in automatically.
  • Other highlighted placeholders, such as [Booking ID], are filled in by clicking them and typing. The same placeholder is filled in on every card that uses it. Press Enter when you are done.
  • The small round reset button on a card clears that card’s placeholders. The reset button in the header clears everything except your agent name.

3. Copy

  • Click (or tap) anywhere on a card to copy it. If a placeholder is still empty, the first empty one is opened for you instead, so you cannot send a script with a gap in it.
  • A small message confirms the copy. Then paste into your chat window.
  • To copy only part of a script, select the text first; selecting does not trigger a copy.

4. Appearance

The half-moon / sun button at the right of the header opens the theme panel. Choose Auto (follows your device), Light or Dark, then a colour theme: Default, Ocean, Sunset or Minimal. Your choice is remembered on this device.

5. Updates never cost you your work

The page checks for a newer version every ten minutes. If there is one, it waits until you have paused typing, saves your entries (names, filled placeholders, search, category and scroll position) inside your browser, reloads, and restores them. You will see a short confirmation. Nothing leaves your device.

Keyboard shortcuts

KeysWhat it does
/Jump to the search box
EscClear the search, close the category rail or the theme panel
Tab / Shift+TabMove between buttons, cards and placeholders
← → ↑ ↓Move between category buttons once one has focus
Enter or SpaceCopy the focused card, or edit the focused placeholder

Troubleshooting

“Failed to copy”

Your browser may be blocking clipboard access. Make sure the page is open over a secure (https) connection, allow clipboard access if asked, or select the text and copy it with Ctrl+C.

The scripts do not load

Check your connection and use the “Try again” button. If it keeps failing, contact us with the browser you use.

A script has a mistake

Tell us on the Contact page with the category and script title and we will correct it.
